## Question

What accounting model, invariants, rounding rules, fee treatment, inventory valuation, authoritative sources, reconciliation states, and repair policies make balances and positions explainable under normal and failure scenarios?

## Comments

The old live ledgers also infer fees and seed from whole-account balances, so they remain failure-scenario sources rather than accounting foundations.

- 2026-07-14: The operator selected paired-lot provenance for canonical grid-cycle results. Paired sells consume their originating buy lots; bootstrap lots are deterministically assigned to initial sell obligations; terminal disposal uses FIFO but remains separate from ordinary cycle profit; retained holdings keep lot provenance.
- 2026-07-14: The operator selected source-exact decimal accounting with no binary-float persistence or generic reconciliation epsilon. Venue rounding occurs only before submission using time-versioned filters and conservative direction; source evidence is never re-rounded; split remainders conserve the original posting; and untradeable residuals remain pending or retained rather than disappearing.
- 2026-07-14: The operator selected native-first valuation with current grid equity and conservative liquidation equity. Costs and fees are attributed exactly once, online owned assets use synchronized best-bid marks, missing/stale valuation remains explicit, performance is flow-adjusted, and risk uses the liquidation view. At the operator's request, the specification now retains the complete recommendation, examples, consequences, and declined alternatives for every accounting choice, including earlier decisions.
- 2026-07-14: The operator selected a versioned, fail-closed invariant suite after every atomic posting batch and reconciliation. It covers native-asset conservation, non-negativity, exclusive ownership classifications, obligation backing, lot provenance, bounded fills, idempotency, actual fee assets, allocation limits, exact valuation identities when available, closed-run cleanup, exact replay, and complete venue-difference classification. Hard violations reject the batch and block new exposure; known in-flight differences may be pending only under explicit evidence and deadlines; unavailable valuation remains distinct and blocks dependent decisions. The specification retains the worked valid-reservation, duplicate-late-fill, and unexplained-fee examples and records why end-only and tolerance-warning alternatives were declined.
- 2026-07-14: The operator selected fact-specific authority rather than treating either the local runtime or Binance as universally authoritative. Immutable configuration governs approved behavior; the journal governs observations, decisions, intents, and attempted commands; authenticated Binance evidence governs venue orders, fills, actual fees, and whole-account custody; the grid subledger governs internal ownership, reservations, and provenance; and recorded market and venue-rule observations govern their historical decision boundaries. Reconciliation items retain both sides and transition through reconciled, pending evidence, venue ahead, local ahead, conflicting evidence, foreign activity, or unexplained difference. The specification preserves the submission-timeout and foreign-sale examples, safety consequences, and reasons universal-local and universal-snapshot alternatives were declined.
- 2026-07-14: The operator selected evidence-preserving, risk-reducing automatic repair. Duplicate deliveries, authenticated missing venue evidence, deterministic projection rebuilds, proven reservation releases, and cancellation of unsupported managed exposure may be handled automatically and idempotently. Ownership, allocation, loss acceptance, foreign-event attribution, retained-holding disposition, and material resume decisions require explicit approval. History rewriting, fabricated evidence, balance-snapshot replacement, tolerance dismissal, uncertain reservation release, uncertain replacement orders, and foreign-as-profit classification are forbidden. Foreign activity remains separate; aggregate per-asset coverage determines whether the grid remains safe or must block exposure. All recommendation details, examples, consequences, and declined alternatives are retained in the specification.

## Answer

The accepted [accounting and reconciliation specification](../../../analysis/accounting-and-reconciliation-spec.md) defines one exact multi-asset grid subledger, paired-lot provenance, source-exact decimals, native fees and dual equity views, a fail-closed invariant suite, fact-specific authority, durable reconciliation states, and evidence-preserving repairs. It preserves internal grid allocation independently from Binance whole-account custody, admits authoritative venue reality without overwriting history, and blocks exposure whenever obligations or allocation coverage cannot be proven. Automatic action is limited to deterministic evidence-backed repair; material ownership, allocation, loss, and foreign-activity decisions remain operator controlled.
