## Answer

Resolved with operator acceptance on 2026-07-15. The canonical [risk model and safety state-machine specification](../../../analysis/risk-and-safety-spec.md) defines one deterministic safety-posture overlay—`NORMAL`, `REDUCE_ONLY`, `FROZEN`, `TERMINAL_LIQUIDATION`, and `CLOSED`—with fail-closed precedence, command permissions, evidence-gated recovery, immutable terminal latching, frozen startup, and bounded planned shutdown.

Economic authority is isolated to an immutable grid capital envelope and exact native-asset allocation. The qualifying-paper and first-live profile is capped at `250 USDT`, including a dynamic fee reserve of at least `5 USDT`; exposure-increasing buys are capped at `20 USDT`, concurrent effective managed orders at 20, and venue capacity preserves the greater of 10 slots or 20% headroom. Rung count remains a jointly validated strategy-search parameter rather than a preselected risk value.

Flow-adjusted conservative liquidation equity governs 2%/`5 USDT` daily loss, 8%/`20 USDT` run drawdown, and the irreversible 12%/`30 USDT` terminal boundary. The exact price stop is jointly validated but must preserve a 2%-of-initial-equity safety buffer above the terminal floor. Terminal disposal uses bounded IOC children; ordinary rungs use bounded `LIMIT_MAKER` placement sequences with no taker fallback.

The answer also fixes worst-case committed inventory, one-order-per-rung occupancy, exact decision-based reconciliation materiality, input-specific stale/control/continuity responses, three-tier anomaly classification, quantitative freshness/reconciliation/clock/dead-man deadlines, risk acceptance cases, fault injection, critical alert requirements, and zero unresolved qualifying-paper differences. Complete recommendations, formulas, scenarios, consequences, assumptions, superseded choices, and declined alternatives remain in the linked specification and domain glossaries.
