Event truth

Partial does not mean provisional.

Every venue-confirmed execution transfers ownership and enters the accounting ledger.

Wrong: wait for completion

Inventory acquired by earlier fills would exist without an accounting entry or paired intent.

Wrong: one sell per fill

Three separate orders fragment occupancy, create duplicated replacement logic, and complicate recovery.

Asset conservation

“The fee” is incomplete without its asset.

The venue reports both quantity and asset; they affect different balances.

Strategy correctness cannot assume BNB fee payment is enabled. It records what Binance actually reports for each fill.

base fee changes the pair

If a buy fills 0.010 BTC but 0.00001 BTC is deducted as fee, selling 0.010 BTC would overstate owned inventory. The cumulative paired sell is based on 0.00999 BTC, subject to venue rounding. The fee remains a separate ledger fact.

Positive spacing is not necessarily positive profit.
