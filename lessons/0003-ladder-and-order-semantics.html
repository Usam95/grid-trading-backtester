Exact meaning

Five rungs means five prices—including both bounds.

It does not mean five intervals, and the activation price is never inserted into the configured geometry.

activation exactly equals a rung

For arithmetic rungs 80, 90, 100, 110, 120 and activation price 100:

  • 80 and 90 initially carry buys.
  • 110 and 120 initially carry sells backed by owned inventory.
  • 100 is the activation rung and begins inactive.
  • It may later receive a paired order after a neighboring fill.

Placing a buy or sell at 100 immediately would erase planned spacing and may execute as taker.

Liquidity intent

Normal rungs must rest as maker orders.

Post-only is an execution constraint, not just a label.

Normal rung

A post-only limit buy at 90 is accepted only if it can enter the book without immediately trading. There is no minimum resting duration: another trader may fill it immediately after acceptance and it still entered as maker liquidity.

Aggressive exception

Bootstrap acquisition and global stop-loss liquidation prioritize completion. Their cost model therefore includes spread, taker fees, and slippage.

a LIMIT_MAKER buy is rejected

The configured buy rung is 90, but the fresh best sell is 89.95. A buy at 90 would take existing liquidity, so Binance rejects it. The engine reconciles the managed order identity, refreshes the order book and venue filters, and may retry below the best sell—but never above the configured 90 rung. It never converts the rung to a taker order.

Retries are bounded by approved attempt, time, and displacement limits. If a bound is exhausted, the rung remains unplaced, the grid enters an exposure-reducing safety pause, and the operator is alerted.

Exchange filters and order states are venue contracts. Production behavior must follow the current Binance Spot trading endpoints and symbol filters.

Invariant

One rung, one cumulative order, one side.

The slot may change side through fills, but it cannot contain separate per-fill orders.

two partial fills share one destination

A buy at 90 fills 0.004 base and later 0.003 base. The destination rung at 100 carries one sell whose cumulative quantity becomes 0.007 base after fee effects—not two sells. This avoids duplicated exposure and makes reconciliation bounded.

Hard boundary

Outer rungs only pair inward.

The static grid never invents a price outside its approved bounds.

Lowest rung at 80 fills

It creates or enlarges a sell at 90. After that sell fills, a replacement buy may return to 80. It never creates a buy at 70.

Highest rung at 120 fills

It creates or enlarges a buy at 110. After that buy fills, a sell may return to 120. It never creates a sell at 130.
