# Mission: Operate a trustworthy grid-trading system

## Why
Build enough practical understanding to judge, validate, and safely operate the personal Binance Spot grid system being specified in this workspace.

## Success looks like
- Explain how capital, inventory, rungs, fills, fees, and profit move through a grid cycle.
- Predict the system state after activation, range exits, pauses, stops, and partial fills.
- Detect configurations that violate exposure, isolation, or positive-cycle requirements.
- Make informed specification decisions without relying on vague trading terminology.

## Constraints
- Begin with spot trading and one active grid on one symbol.
- Prefer concrete examples, interactive feedback, and concise canonical definitions.
- Treat backtests as evidence, not investment guarantees.

## Out of scope
- Futures, options, leverage, borrowing, shorting, and multi-strategy portfolio allocation.
